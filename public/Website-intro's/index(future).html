Most creative people work alone, start strong, and stall out.

Multimedia Club is the room where that stops happening. You bring the rough cut, the half-finished poster, the idea you’ve been sitting on. We bring the people who’ll push it over the line.

What you get

Feedback that’s actually useful

Not “looks great,” but notes you can act on. Structured critiques from people who make things too.

Collaborators, on demand

Find an editor, a composer, or a second pair of eyes in a day — not a cold DM into the void.

A deadline with teeth

Monthly club projects ship whether you’re ready or not. That’s the whole trick.

Three steps. No gatekeeping.

Show up. Join a project or pitch your own. Build together. Weekly work sessions, open critiques, shared resources. Ship it. Everything gets finished and shown.
